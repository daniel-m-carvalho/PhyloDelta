## Contradictions and open points

* **Phylo.io cannot re-root a tree it has compared** (Table 24's note). Its own "Reroot" makes the
  call the harness makes, and it throws in compare mode — at 1,000 leaves while redrawing
  (`colorScale.node is not a function`), from 2,500 when the comparison restarts and the re-rooted
  model is posted to its worker (`Maximum call stack size exceeded`). Outside compare mode the same
  call works, checked by hand. So Table 24 has no phylo.io time: the one interaction that makes both
  tools redo the comparison is the one phylo.io does not complete.
* **The jump's failing clades vary in number between loads**: 22, 22, 1, 22, 22 at 1,000 leaves,
  because phylo.io's comparison is not deterministic. The earlier "eight largest" came from fewer
  loads (DECISIONS, Corrections).
* **PhyloDelta's re-root below 35,290 leaves is set by the worker's 2 s poll**, not by the tree: the
  five runs at 2,500 spread 0.73–2.56 s. Above it the spread is under 0.1%.
* **Still open from v0.1.0**: whether phylo.io's comparison above 10,000 is slow or stuck. It does not
  finish in 30 minutes at 17,645, 35,290 or 70,580 in any of five loads.
