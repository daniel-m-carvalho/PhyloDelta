## Contradictions and open points

The script checks found no contradiction between Tables 1, 2, 3, 7, 8, 9 and 23. These remain:

* **PhyloDelta's reading after a phylo.io attempt.** One ceiling pass read up to 1.35 s at 70,580 and a
  second 0.26 s, with the same overlap. It is unexplained. Table 1 uses the median of six and says so.
* **Is the comparison slow or stuck?** 201 s at 10,000 to over 1,800 s at 17,645 is much steeper than the
  3x to 4x per doubling below it, and 17,645 is the first rung with an unshared leaf. Untested. The
  thesis should say "did not finish in 30 minutes", not "cannot finish".
* **Clade counts, 2,493 against 2,498.** Not a difference in the trees' structure: both sides have 2,499
  clades at 2,500 leaves and all join. The count is how many phylo.io scored, and over five runs it was
  2,497–2,498, the root never being scored. Its comparison is random, which is the likely reason, but
  2,493 itself was not reproduced, and that run's pair id differs.
* **The jump's mechanism** in §34.14 fits all-or-nothing loads better than the mixed load now seen. It is
  marked as likely, not established.
* **Depth is resolved:** 79–207 in the NJ trees and 68–474 in the UPGMA trees, the real pair 176 and 443.

## What departs from the brief

* **Chrome updated itself** 79 seconds into the campaign, before any browser step. `campaign_env.py`
  refused at the end on `browser`, and that stands in `environment.json`.
* **`ceiling` hung** after its 660 s deadline. Its Chrome was stopped by hand and the rungs from 70,580 up
  re-measured, so the campaign's first pass is kept apart in `ceiling_first_pass.json`.
* **Four checks are outside the campaign**, same tag, own files: 2,500 repeats, paint at 70,580, 30 minutes
  at 70,580 and 564,640, and the comparison given 30 minutes. The last is `endurance.mjs` with the budget
  passed, which is not at the tag, so that one runner is a one-line variant.
* **`endurance.mjs` reported `70,580 COMPLETED in 675s` falsely.** `compareComplete` was false. Table 4 reads
  that field. It and `ceiling.mjs` keep their defects at the tag, and are fixed after it (§34.21).
* **Not re-measured:** `mast_memory.json` and `thread_builds_2_hot.json`, which the campaign does not
  produce, and the mid-sized-clade breakdown of misses in §34.4.
* **Not edited:** the thesis LaTeX. `CLAUDE.md` still says accuracy reaches 5,000 and phylo.io is n=1.
